about me

2026/5/4

Hi, this is Hico (Atakohico)!

Currently pursuing a degree in Computer Animation and Visual Effects at Bournemouth University, I am a visual artist dedicated to the intersection of 2D concept design and 3D technology. My work is defined by vibrant, high-recognition color palettes and a versatile stylistic range—from stylized cartoons to realistic proportions.


Professional Skills

3D: Proficient in Maya and ZBrush, with a solid foundation in modeling, digital sculpting, and rigging. I specialize in 3D-assisted illustration and character asset creation.

2D: Advanced user of PS, SAI2, and Procreate. Currently expanding expertise into CSP and After Effects for motion graphics and sophisticated line-art workflows.

Design & Concept: Strong focus on Character Concept Art and Graphic Design.


CV & Experience

Education:

BA (Hons) Computer Animation and Visual Effects | Bournemouth University (UK)

As an Independent Illustrator, I Successfully curated and sold self-produced art merchandise at multiple mid-sized ACG conventions. Managed the entire pipeline from concept design and manufacturing to onsite marketing.

Also, I am founder of a personal online art store, handling brand visual identity, customer engagement, and high-quality artistic deliverables.


Personal Development Plan

Strategic Goals:

Short-term (1 Year): Mastery of motion graphics (AE) and advanced illustration fundamentals. Bridging the gap between static art and dynamic visual storytelling.

Long-term (3-5 Years): Establishing a global creative presence via X and YouTube; securing professional internships/roles in the concept art industry to refine industrial-level production skills.
